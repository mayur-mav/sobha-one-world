— THE PROJECT —

Sobha One World Greater Whitefield,
Project Overview

An iconic high-rise township comprising 14 towers soaring up to 46 storeys, set across 48 lush acres in Phase 1 with panoramic viewing balconies, 4 grand themed clubhouses, and resort-style crystal water lagoons. Conceived with SOBHA's signature German backward integration, world-class sports courts, and direct access to Old Madras Road (NH-75) and STRR.

A self-sustaining master township oasis: Over 80% of the Phase 1 expanse is dedicated open landscape, planned as resort crystal water lagoons, Zen Japanese gardens, and tree-lined boulevards with zero surface traffic.
14 Iconic towers rising 46 storeys: Modern monolithic architecture engineered with in-house precast systems, featuring 10-foot ceilings, imported marble flooring, deep viewing decks, and high-speed elevators.
Four ways to live here: From 734 sq ft 1 BHK luxury residences to expansive 2,415+ sq ft 4 BHK Presidential suites, each residence engineered with Vastu principles and panoramic open skyline vistas.
Four themed clubhouses spanning 1,00,000+ sq ft: Indoor squash, Olympic lap pool, crystal lagoon, world-class gymnasium, luxury spa, business pods, and private mini-screening lounges.
The premier growth corridor of East Bengaluru: Located directly on NH-75 / Old Madras Road, 3 mins to STRR (NH 648), 18 mins to Kadugodi Metro, 20 mins to Whitefield ITPL, and 35 mins to Kempegowda Airport.
